# Client Management Toolkit (Professional Grade)

This toolkit provides the operational framework for managing clients using the Concierge Model. It ensures professional boundaries, clear expectations, and a consistent client experience.

## 1. Client Intake & Onboarding
### Intake Checklist
Use this list during your first call to ensure you have everything needed for the workspace.
- [ ] **Full Legal Name** (as it appears on credit reports)
- [ ] **Current Mailing Address** (for draft letters)
- [ ] **Email Address** (for delivery of drafts)
- [ ] **Redacted Credit Reports** (PDF/PNG/JPG from Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, Innovis)
- [ ] **Supporting Evidence** (Payment confirmations, closing statements, identity docs)
- [ ] **Prior Dispute History** (Dates and outcomes of previous disputes for the same items)

### Redaction Guide (Security)
*Send this to the client to ensure they don't send you sensitive data you don't need.*

**How to Securely Prepare Your Documents:**
To protect your identity, please redact your documents before uploading them to my workspace:
1. **SSN**: Use a black marker or digital "black-out" tool to cover the first 5 digits of your Social Security Number. **Leave the last 4 digits visible**—I need these to match the items in the app.
2. **Account Numbers**: Cover all but the last 4 digits of any account number.
3. **Passwords/PINs**: Completely black out any passwords, security questions, or PINs.
4. **Check**: If you can see through the marker when holding the paper to light, use a darker pen or double-layer the redaction.

## 3. Execution & Delivery
### "How to Submit" Client Guide
Include this with your PDF drafts.

**Guide: Submitting Your Disputes**
Now that you have your professional drafts, follow these steps:

**Option A: Online Submission (Fastest)**
1. Log in to the official bureau portal.
2. Copy the text from the PDF drafts into the online form.
3. Upload the supporting documents I have flagged.

**Option B: Written Mail (Most Formal)**
1. Print the PDF drafts.
2. Sign each letter in ink.
3. Attach photocopies of supporting documents.
4. **Identity Proof**: Include a copy of your government ID and a utility bill.
5. **Mail**: Use **Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested**. Keep your tracking numbers!

## 4. Long-Term Management
### Communication Cadence (The Experience)
To prevent client anxiety, follow this "touch-point" schedule:

**The Friday Update (Weekly):**
Even if there is no news from the bureau, send this every Friday:
*"Hi [Name], just checking your portal. The bureau is still investigating. No action needed from you today. I'll update you again next Friday."*

**The Response Update (Immediate):**
As soon as a response is recorded in the app:
*"Hi [Name], the bureau has responded. I've analyzed the result—they have [deleted/verified] the item. I've updated your tracker. Let's discuss the next step."*

### The Escalation Map (The Strategy)
When a bureau refuses to delete an item ("Verified"), move to the next phase. This allows you to offer a "Phase 2" service.

**Phase 1: Direct Bureau Dispute**
- Prepare draft $\rightarrow$ Client submits $\rightarrow$ Bureau investigates.
- *If Verified* $\rightarrow$ Move to Phase 2.

**Phase 2: Furnisher Dispute (Direct)**
- Dispute the item directly with the bank/creditor (the furnisher).
- *If Verified* $\rightarrow$ Move to Phase 3.

**Phase 3: Federal Escalation (CFPB/BBB)**
- Use the "Complaints" section of the app to draft a CFPB or BBB complaint.
- Attach the results of Phase 1 and 2 to prove the bureau failed to investigate properly.
- *Result*: Final bureau response.
